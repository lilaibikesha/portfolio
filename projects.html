Application Planner

Live

A mobile-first planner that helps high school students plan their Minerva application, with a start-by date for every step counted back from their deadline.

My role: Product owner & builder, working with an AI coding agent · Minerva Admissions AI Research Team, Nov 2026

  • HTML
  • CSS
  • JavaScript
  • JSON data model
  • GitHub Pages & Actions

The build

  • No-framework, no-build static site. Every step, deadline and tip lives in one data.json that all pages read.
  • Scheduler that skips past days and the student's busy days, with waiting steps scheduled first.
  • Deployed with GitHub Actions to GitHub Pages.

Problem & decisions

  • Users: applicants, including students at schools without a counselor, who don't know how long each part takes.
  • Only states what Minerva's official pages say, and sends everything else to admissions.
  • Privacy by design: runs in the browser, saves nothing, no tracking.

Impact

  • Goal: help applicants finish every part of their application before the deadline.
