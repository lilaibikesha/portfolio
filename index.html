Application Planner

A mobile-first planner that helps high school students plan their Minerva application. It works out a start-by date for every step, counting back from their deadline.

My role: Product owner & builder, working with an AI coding agent · Minerva Admissions AI Research Team, Nov 2026

  • HTML
  • CSS
  • JavaScript
  • JSON data model
  • GitHub Pages & Actions

The build

  • No-framework, no-build static site. Every step, deadline, and tip lives in one data.json that all pages (and a future AI helper) read.
  • Client-side scheduler that skips past days and the student's busy days, with waiting steps (school records, translations) scheduled first.
  • Deployed with GitHub Actions to GitHub Pages.

Problem & decisions

  • Users: applicants, including students at schools without a counselor, who don't know how long each part takes.
  • Only states what Minerva's official pages say, and sends everything else to admissions.
  • Privacy by design: runs in the browser, saves nothing, no tracking.

Business impact

  • Goal: help applicants finish every part of their application before the deadline.
